# FINTRAC Mortgage Review-Readiness: Launch Plan

**Status:** Plan for a separately authorized five-business-day field test. Nothing here has been executed. All field results are UNTESTED.
**As-of date:** September 6, 2026.

Neurolabs is not affiliated with or endorsed by FINTRAC or FSRA. Readiness support is not the prescribed effectiveness review. Independent review work is performed by the named AML practitioner, and the reporting entity remains responsible for its obligations.

## 1. Five-business-day field-validation plan

Preconditions before day one: founder authorization; counsel booked for a one-hour review of the responsibility matrix, disclaimer, and contract structure; a tracker created per the [validation assets](FINTRAC-MORTGAGE-VALIDATION-ASSETS.md) schema.

| Day | Activities | Output | Gate check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send partnership brief to 3 to 5 candidate AML practitioners. Begin the 100-entity list from the FSRA public register and Ontario Business Registry. Counsel review of wording. | 40+ entities listed; 3+ practitioner conversations booked | Practitioner pipeline started |
| 2 | Finish list to 100. Vet practitioners with the checklist; request written quotes for the standard small-brokerage profile. Send Message A to the first 40 entities. | 100 entities; 1+ written quote requested | List gate: 100 identified |
| 3 | Send Message A to the remaining 60. Hold discovery calls as booked. Confirm at least one practitioner's insurance certificate and capacity. | 5+ conversations; 1 practitioner provisionally vetted | Practitioner gate progress |
| 4 | Follow-up Message B to non-responders. Discovery calls. Offer the Evidence Readiness Map to qualified prospects. | 10+ conversations; first paid diagnostic possible | Demand signal |
| 5 | Final calls. Score the tracker. Compute conversation, qualification, and paid conversion rates. Write the go/no-go memo. | 15+ conversations target; scorecard completed | All gates scored |

Rules during the test: no landing page build (use the one-page PDF), no payment processor beyond an existing invoice tool, no borrower data, no readiness work started until a practitioner is vetted and a contract exists.

## 4. Unit economics and structure comparison

### 4.1 Contribution sensitivity for the standard sprint (CAD)

| Neurolabs hours | Cost at 87/hour | Contribution at 2,500 | Contribution at 2,950 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 870 | 1,630 | 2,080 |
| 11.5 | 1,000 | 1,500 | 1,950 |
| 14 | 1,220 | 1,280 | 1,730 |
| 16 | 1,390 | 1,110 | 1,560 |

The CAD 1,500 gate holds only at 11.5 hours or fewer at CAD 2,500. Recommendation: cap scope in writing at 12 hours, or price the standard sprint at CAD 2,950 after the first three clients. Below CAD 1,000 contribution (about 17 hours at CAD 2,500) is a NO-GO signal.

### 4.2 Structure comparison

| Criterion | 1. Separate contracts and invoices | 2. Neurolabs prime, specialist subcontractor | 3. Referral only |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liability | Each party liable for its own scope. Best. | Neurolabs carries the review on its contract. Worst. | Neurolabs liable for referral representations only. |
| Professional insurance | Practitioner's E&O covers the review; Neurolabs needs general and E&O for admin services. | Neurolabs' E&O would need to cover AML review work it cannot perform. Likely unavailable or costly. [UNKNOWN] | Minimal. |
| Conflicts and independence | Clear. Practitioner independent of Neurolabs and of program authoring. | Practitioner looks like Neurolabs staff; independence optics weaker. | Clear. |
| Customer clarity | Two invoices, two scopes. Requires explanation. | One invoice. Simplest for buyer but blurs who performs the review. | Clear but Neurolabs adds little. |
| Margin | Neurolabs keeps its full fee. No markup on the review. | Markup possible but margin exposed to practitioner overruns. | Referral fee only, if any, and must be disclosed. |
| Sales friction | Medium. Two signatures. | Low. | Low, but no product. |
| Tax | HST on each invoice. Accountant to confirm. | HST on combined; subcontractor input credits. | HST on referral fee if any. |
| Data controller | Neurolabs is processor of metadata only; practitioner controls review records. Clean. | Neurolabs becomes responsible for data it should not hold. | None. |

**Recommendation:** structure 1, with a written coordination agreement. Structure 3 is the fallback if no practitioner accepts coordination terms. Structure 2 is rejected. Final terms require counsel; do not draft them from this document.
