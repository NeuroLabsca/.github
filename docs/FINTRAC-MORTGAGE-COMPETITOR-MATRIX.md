## 3. Observed white space

[INFERENCE from the matrix.]

1. **No priced, fixed-scope readiness product for small mortgage brokerages.** Every reviewer bundles preparation. Every software vendor stops at records. Networks supply scaffolding but not the review.
2. **No provider explains the responsibility split in plain language.** Provider pages say "we conduct your review" or "our tool keeps you compliant". A page that shows who does what, with two invoices, would be distinctive.
3. **Price opacity.** Only C&G publishes ranges, and not for mortgage. A published Neurolabs price and a published specialist quote process would stand out.
4. **Timing service.** No provider advertises coordination against the 30-day senior-officer memo or the 24-month next-cycle start date.

Limits of the white space:

- A cheap solo reviewer at CAD 3,000 all-in can undercut "readiness + review".
- The AML Shop's network relationships may already cover a meaningful share of franchise brokerages. [UNKNOWN]
- Real estate bundles at Fintracker's price point could be adapted to mortgage by that vendor quickly.
- Neurolabs has no AML track record; credibility depends entirely on the named specialist.
