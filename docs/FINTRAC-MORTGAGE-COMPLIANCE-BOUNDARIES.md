# FINTRAC Mortgage Review-Readiness: Compliance Boundaries

**Status:** Exploration draft. Not legal advice. Requires review by Canadian AML counsel and the named AML specialist before use in any contract or marketing.
**As-of date:** September 6, 2026. Sources accessed September 6, 2026.

Neurolabs is not affiliated with or endorsed by FINTRAC or FSRA. Readiness support is not the prescribed effectiveness review. Independent review work is performed by the named AML practitioner, and the reporting entity remains responsible for its obligations.

Regulatory basis for the split: the review must test the effectiveness of policies and procedures, risk assessment, and training, be carried out and documented every two years by an internal or external auditor or by the entity itself, and be reported to a senior officer within 30 days ([PCMLTFR s. 156](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-184/section-156.html); [FINTRAC guidance, modified 2024-10-11](https://fintrac-canafe.canada.ca/guidance-directives/compliance-conformite/guide4/4-eng)). FINTRAC's best practice is that the reviewer not be directly involved in the compliance program ([FINTRAC video, modified 2025-02-20](https://fintrac-canafe.canada.ca/training-formation/mortgage-hypotheque/video-eng)).

## 2. Permitted and prohibited Neurolabs activities

### Permitted

- Explain, in plain language and with links, what FINTRAC's public guidance says, without applying it to the client's facts.
- Build and maintain the evidence index and remediation tracker.
- Ask the client whether a document exists, who owns it, and when it was last updated.
- Prepare interview schedules, agendas provided by the specialist, and logistics.
- Format the specialist's report and the senior-officer memo to the specialist's content.
- Track dates: review start, completion, 30-day memo due date, remediation deadlines, next-cycle start (24 months from this review's start per FINTRAC guidance).
- Tell a client that a question is "one for the reviewer" and log it as Needs reviewer judgment.

### Prohibited

- Determining whether the entity is a reporting entity or which mortgage role applies.
- Stating whether any document, policy, control, or training is adequate, compliant, or effective.
- Choosing samples, review periods, or testing methods.
- Reading borrower files, identity documents, transaction records, or loan files.
- Opining on whether a transaction was suspicious or whether an STR should have been filed.
- Drafting or editing policies, procedures, risk assessments, or training content.
- Backdating, re-dating, or "cleaning up" any record. If a document lacks a date or approval, log it as Missing or Needs reviewer judgment.
- Signing, co-signing, or being named as the reviewer on any review report.
- Describing Neurolabs' work as a review, audit, assessment of compliance, certification, or approval.
- Contacting FINTRAC or FSRA on the client's behalf.

## 3. Data-handling model

### 3.1 Principle

Raw borrower files stay in a client-controlled or specialist-controlled secure data room. Neurolabs works from metadata only. This is consistent with PIPEDA's safeguards principle, which requires protection proportional to sensitivity and recommends removing irrelevant personal information from documents shared with others and need-to-know access ([OPC, PIPEDA safeguards, modified 2025-08-11](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/p_principle/principles/p_safeguards/)).

### 3.2 Minimum data Neurolabs needs

| Field | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Client legal name, licence number, province, contact for the principal broker (business email and phone) | | Engagement administration |
| Document name and version | "AML Policies and Procedures v2.1" | Evidence index |
| Presence or absence | Present / Missing | Evidence index |
| Responsible owner (role, business email) | "Principal broker" | Follow-up |
| Relevant date | Approval date, last review date | Currency tracking |
| Secure-location reference | "Client SharePoint > Compliance > Policies" (folder path or data-room label; never a credential-bearing or public URL) | Retrieval by specialist |
| Workflow status | Present / Missing / Needs reviewer judgment | Tracking |
| Remediation deadline and owner | | Tracking |
| File-universe totals | "Review period Oct 11, 2024 to Sep 30, 2026: 212 files arranged, 18 renewals" | Scheduling and scope |
| Internal file IDs, if the specialist asks Neurolabs to track sample status | "F-2025-0117" | Only if IDs contain no personal information |
| Staff names and roles for interview scheduling | Business contact details only | Scheduling |

### 3.3 Prohibited data

Borrower or guarantor names, addresses, dates of birth, identification documents or numbers, credit reports, bank statements, income documents, loan applications, mortgage commitments, transaction details, STR or other report contents, beneficial-ownership records, PEP screening results, and any exported list that contains any of the above. If a client sends any of this, Neurolabs follows the incident procedure in section 3.7.

### 3.4 Collection, access, retention, deletion

- **Collection:** through a structured intake form or spreadsheet that has no file-upload field. Email attachments are discouraged; any attachment is checked before opening for signs of prohibited data (file names such as "application", "ID", "statement").
- **Access:** role-based. Coordinator (full index), formatter (report text supplied by specialist only), founder (oversight). Specialist has read access to the index. Client has read access to its own index.
- **Retention:** engagement metadata retained for 24 months after engagement close to support the next cycle, then deleted, unless the client asks for earlier deletion. Period to be confirmed with counsel and aligned with the client's own retention duties.
- **Deletion:** documented deletion on request or at the retention date. Formatted report drafts deleted after the specialist confirms the signed final.
- **Breach expectations:** any unauthorized access, loss, or receipt of prohibited data is logged, the client and specialist are notified the same business day, and PIPEDA breach-of-security-safeguards assessment is triggered. Counsel confirms notification duties.

### 3.5 Role-based access

| Role | Evidence index | Report drafts | Client contacts | Borrower data |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Neurolabs coordinator | Read/write | Format only | Yes | Never |
| Neurolabs founder | Read | Read | Yes | Never |
| AML specialist | Read | Author | Yes | Yes, in the client or specialist data room |
| Client compliance officer | Read own | Receives final | | Owns |

### 3.6 Approved secure-transfer patterns

- Client-controlled data room or file share where the specialist is granted access by the client; Neurolabs is not granted access.
- Specialist-controlled data room with client upload; Neurolabs is not granted access.
- Index and tracker in a Neurolabs workspace with two-factor authentication, shared with named users only; no public links.
- Report drafts exchanged only between specialist and Neurolabs through an authenticated channel, with no borrower identifiers in the text. The specialist redacts before sending anything for formatting.

Not approved: public or unlisted links, consumer messaging apps, unencrypted email attachments of any record, screen-sharing of borrower files to Neurolabs.

### 3.7 Incident escalation

1. Stop. Do not open, forward, or index the item.
2. Log time, sender, description (no content), and who saw it.
3. Notify the client's compliance officer and the specialist the same business day.
4. Delete or return per the client's instruction, and record the deletion.
5. Founder reviews within two business days and decides whether counsel is needed.

### 3.8 Contract and privacy clauses requiring professional review

- Scope definition stating that Neurolabs performs administrative readiness support only and does not perform the prescribed review.
- No-PII clause with the client's undertaking not to send prohibited data and the incident procedure.
- Data-controller and processor roles and PIPEDA safeguards.
- Limitation of liability proportionate to fees, and exclusion of regulatory penalties.
- Non-reliance clause: Neurolabs' index is not an opinion on compliance.
- Coordination agreement between Neurolabs and the specialist covering naming rights, referral disclosure, no fee-splitting unless disclosed to the client, confidentiality, insurance minimums, and conflicts.
- Cancellation, scope-change, and examination-notice suspension terms.
- Tax treatment of separate invoices (HST on each). Accountant to confirm.

## 4. Claims rules

### Prohibited claims

- "FINTRAC approved", "FINTRAC registered provider", "FINTRAC endorsed"
- "Certified compliant", "compliance certificate"
- "Guaranteed to pass"
- "Penalty proof", "audit proof"
- "Neurolabs reviews your compliance program"
- "Complete your FINTRAC review with Neurolabs"
- "File your review by October 11, 2026" or any wording implying a filing
- Any statement that a mortgage broker has been fined for a missing review, unless FINTRAC publishes one. As of the AMP list modified 2026-07-09, none exists ([FINTRAC AMP list](https://fintrac-canafe.canada.ca/pen/4-eng)). Real estate examples must be labelled as real estate.

### Required disclaimer (every page, proposal, and report cover)

> Neurolabs is not affiliated with or endorsed by FINTRAC or FSRA. Readiness support is not the prescribed effectiveness review. Independent review work is performed by the named AML practitioner, and the reporting entity remains responsible for its obligations.

### Required vocabulary

- Use "review readiness", "evidence readiness", "readiness sprint", "effectiveness review" (for the statutory review, with FINTRAC's terminology preserved).
- Do not brand the Neurolabs offer as an "audit". When quoting the Regulations, keep "internal or external auditor" verbatim.
- Refer to "FINTRAC's stated expectation" for the October 11, 2026 date, never "deadline to file".

## 5. Positioning: where buyers could be confused, and fixes

Tested line: "Neurolabs organizes the evidence and delivery process; a named Canadian AML specialist performs the testing and reaches the professional conclusions."

| Confusion risk | Why it arises | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer thinks the review is included in the Neurolabs price. | One landing page, one CTA, one price list. | Show two boxes and two invoices on the offer page: "Readiness Sprint (Neurolabs, CAD 2,500)" and "Effectiveness Review (specialist, quoted separately by the specialist)". |
| Buyer thinks the specialist is Neurolabs staff. | "Our specialist" language. | Always "an independent AML practitioner, [Name], engaged by you directly". Never "our". |
| Buyer thinks the evidence index is an assessment. | "Readiness map" sounds like a verdict. | Label it "Evidence Readiness Map: what exists, what is missing, what needs the reviewer's judgment. It is not an opinion on compliance." |
| Buyer thinks FINTRAC recognizes the service. | Regulator name in the headline. | Disclaimer directly under the headline, not only in the footer. |
| Buyer thinks October 11 is a filing date. | "Deadline" wording. | "FINTRAC expects first reviews completed before October 11, 2026. Nothing is filed; the review is documented and reported to your senior officer within 30 days." |
| Reviewer independence questioned. | Neurolabs coordinates, tracks remediation, and markets the specialist. | Neurolabs never authors program documents. Specialist's engagement letter states independence. Counsel confirms. |

Recommended replacement subhead:

> Neurolabs organizes your documents, evidence list, file counts, and interview schedule. An independent Canadian AML practitioner, engaged by you directly, performs the testing and reaches the conclusions. Neurolabs does not perform the effectiveness review.

## 6. Examination-notice escalation

Trigger: the prospect or client states, at any point, that it has received a FINTRAC examination notice, is under examination, has received a notice of violation, or is in a compliance agreement.

Procedure:

1. **Preserve.** Instruct the client, in writing, to preserve all existing records as they are.
2. **No changes.** No backdating, re-dating, rewriting, re-approving, or "tidying" of any policy, log, record, or training file. Log this instruction.
3. **Stop.** Suspend the readiness workflow. Freeze the evidence index at its current state and record the freeze time.
4. **Escalate the same business day** to the named AML specialist and recommend the client consult Canadian AML counsel. Neurolabs does not advise on the examination.
5. **Resume only** on written instruction from the specialist or counsel, under a revised scope.
6. During the fit check, a "yes" to the examination question routes the prospect to this procedure before any other discussion.

Basis: FINTRAC examiners assess whether the review was in fact conducted and documented ([assessment manual, July 2025](https://fintrac-canafe.canada.ca/guidance-directives/exam-examen/cam/cams-eng)); records altered after a notice would expose the client and Neurolabs to serious risk. [INFERENCE]
